These terms explain the rules for using Coffee Lab: creating an account, building drinks, placing orders, and sharing recipes. By using Coffee Lab you agree to them. Coffee Lab operates in the Philippines. This page is a plain-language description of how the service works, not legal advice. How we handle personal information is explained in our Privacy Policy.

1. Your account

  • Keep your password and recovery codes private. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • Give accurate details when ordering (name, phone, and address for delivery).
  • Tell us right away if you think someone else has used your account.

2. Orders and payment

  • The price shown at checkout is calculated by our server and is the amount due. Payment is in cash on pickup or on delivery; no online payment is taken.
  • Delivery and pickup times shown are estimates.
  • We may decline or cancel an order, for example if an address cannot be reached or an order looks fraudulent. Nothing is charged for a cancelled order.
  • Nutrition and caffeine values are estimates based on standard recipes.

3. Community recipes and loyalty points

  • When you publish a recipe, its name, the drink, and your username become visible to anyone with the link or QR code until you unpublish it. Only publish names you have the right to use and that are not offensive.
  • By publishing, you let Coffee Lab show the recipe and let other customers order it. You can unpublish at any time from the publish dialog.
  • Recipe royalty points are a loyalty reward with no cash value. They count only after the buyer's order is paid, are not earned on your own orders, and may be adjusted if they were earned through misuse.

4. Caffeine guardian

The caffeine guardian shows estimates and general guidance only. It is not medical advice. If you are pregnant, have a health condition, or are sensitive to caffeine, follow your doctor's advice.

5. Acceptable use

Please don't:

  • try to access other people's accounts, orders, or our admin tools;
  • interfere with the service, for example by automated or excessive requests;
  • place fake orders or use recipes and points to mislead or harass anyone.

We may suspend accounts that break these rules.
