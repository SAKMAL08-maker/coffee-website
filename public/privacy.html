Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This notice explains how Coffee Lab collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you create an account, customize drinks, place orders, or contact us. Coffee Lab operates in the Philippines and aims to handle personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and applicable guidance. This page is a description of current practices, not legal advice.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: username and account-security records, including password and recovery-code hashes. We do not need your plain-text password to sign you in.
  • Order details: customer name and phone number, selected drinks and order configuration, order history and status, and any notes you choose to provide.
  • Delivery details: delivery address when you choose delivery. If you choose to use your device's location feature, approximate or precise device coordinates may also be sent with the order.
  • Receipt records: generated order receipts and associated preview images.

Please avoid putting passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information in order notes.

2. How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, prepare and manage orders, coordinate delivery or pickup, generate receipts, show order history and status, respond to support or privacy requests, and protect the service against misuse. We do not describe optional location access as required for account creation; you can enter a delivery address manually.

3. Where information is stored

  • Supabase: stores Coffee Lab application records, including account and order data.
  • Backblaze: stores generated receipt files and receipt preview images.
  • Render: hosts the Coffee Lab web application and processes requests needed to operate it.

These service providers process information as part of delivering the service. Access to personal information should be limited to people and systems that need it to operate or support Coffee Lab.

4. Location and optional information

Location is optional. If you use the location button, your browser or device may ask for permission and the resulting coordinates may be used to help with delivery details. You can decline location permission and type your address instead. Email is not required by the current checkout flow.

5. Sharing

We use the service providers listed above to run the application and store its records. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when necessary to respond to a lawful request, protect users or Coffee Lab, investigate misuse, or meet applicable legal obligations. Only provide information needed for your order.

6. Retention and deletion

Coffee Lab does not currently have a fixed, published retention period. Order records and receipt files are cleaned up when necessary. We are not promising that every record is deleted immediately on request; some information may need to be retained for legitimate operational, security, dispute-resolution, or legal and accounting reasons. We should review and formalize a retention schedule as our operations mature.

There is not currently a self-service account or data deletion feature. To request access to your information, correction, deletion, or help with another privacy concern, email us using the contact details below. We will review the request and explain any applicable limits or next steps.

7. Security

We use measures such as access controls and application security protections intended to help safeguard information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password and account recovery codes private, and contact us if you believe your account or information has been compromised.

8. Your privacy requests

For privacy questions or requests about personal information, contact coffeelab08@gmail.com. Please describe your request and include enough information for us to locate the relevant account or order. Do not email your password, recovery codes, or other secrets.

Requests will be assessed under applicable Philippine privacy law, including any applicable exceptions or obligations. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this page as Coffee Lab's features or practices change. The effective date at the top indicates when this version was prepared. Please check this page periodically for updates.

Questions? coffeelab08@gmail.com
